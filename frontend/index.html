TreadTrace F1-SPEC AI

Tyre Intelligence & 4-Wheel Digital Twin Platform

AI Motorsport Intelligence

TreadTrace isolates true, inferred tyre-related performance degradation from confounding motorsport practice and race variables. In competitive motorsport telemetry, naive lap time regression conflates tyre decay with vehicle mass loss, track grip progression, and traffic disruptions.

By explicitly separating tyre effects from race-progress fuel proxies, ambient and track evolution, and traffic classification, TreadTrace exposes true compound decay dynamics without relying on intrusive or unavailable physical rubber wear measurements.

Methodology, 2022–2025 F1 Data & Temporal Holdout

TreadTrace is calibrated and evaluated on 2022–2025 Formula 1 timing and telemetry data, with 2025 held out as a strict temporal holdout benchmark. This ensures the confounder isolation algorithms and robust Huber regression formulations generalize reliably across seasons, circuits, and evolving aerodynamic regulations without data leakage.

Core Analytical Modules

  • 4-Wheel Digital Twin: Stateful per-corner digital twin tracking thermal accumulation cycles, inferred wear states, and remaining useful life (RUL) projections.
  • Degradation Analysis: Confounder-aware robust regression separating tyre degradation curves from lap-to-lap noise and race-progress fuel proxies.
  • Tyre Memory: Closed-loop Bayesian calibration storing cross-session compound baselines and historical decay rates.
  • Forward Prediction: Stint simulation forecasting compound crossover laps and strategy windows under varying track temperature scenarios.
  • Validation & Root-Cause Attribution: Post-session comparison quantifying error reduction against naive models and explaining real-world performance variance.
  • AI Evidence: Explainable telemetry decomposition and mathematical audit trails supporting race engineering decisions.
